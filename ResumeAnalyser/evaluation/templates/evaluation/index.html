Job Description

Paste the role requirements, responsibilities, experience expectations, skills, location, and work mode. This becomes the scoring reference for every resume.

Required

Good job descriptions include

  • Job title, seniority, location, and work mode.
  • Required skills separated from preferred skills.
  • Experience, education, responsibilities, and hiring priorities.
